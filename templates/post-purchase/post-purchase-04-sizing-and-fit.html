How merino should sit

The right fit depends on where the piece sits in your layering — and it’s simpler than it sounds.

Base layers

Close to the skin

A base layer should sit snug against your body — close, not tight. That contact is what lets the fibre read your temperature and wick moisture, so it keeps you warm and dry. Go for your true size here; if it feels like a second skin, it’s right.

Mid-layers & tops

Room to move & layer

Worn over a base layer or on its own, a mid-layer or everyday top should fall with a little ease — relaxed, with space to move and layer underneath. Your true size usually works; size up if you like a looser, roomier drape.

When to size up — and when not to

Three quick rules take the guesswork out of your next order.

It’s true to size for most people

Order your usual size and you’ll be right most of the time. Between two sizes? Size down for a snug base-layer feel, or up for a relaxed everyday fit — the size chart has your exact measurements either way.

Size up if you layer heavily

Planning to wear a hoodie or mid-layer over thicker clothes, or simply prefer extra room? Go one size up. For a top worn straight against the skin, stay true to size so it keeps that close, warming contact.

It holds its size — when washed right

The size you order is the size you keep. Wash cold on a gentle cycle and lay flat to dry — skip the hot wash and tumble dryer, and merino won’t shrink or lose its shape. It’s all in your care guide if you want a refresher.
