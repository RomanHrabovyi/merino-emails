Make it last

Merino looks after you — here’s how to look after it

Good news: it’s less work than cotton. Three habits keep it soft and fresh for years.

 

Wash less, wash cool

Merino resists odour, so it rarely needs washing. When it does, use a cold, gentle wool cycle.
 

Lay flat to dry

Skip the dryer. Reshape and dry flat to keep the fit exactly how you like it.
 

Air it between wears

Hang it overnight and it’s ready to go again — most days a breather is all it needs.
