Make it last

Caring for merino is easier than you’d think

Three habits that keep your merino soft, fresh and in shape for years.

1

Wash less, wash cool

Merino resists odour on its own, so it needs washing far less often than cotton. When you do, use a cold, gentle wool cycle.
2

Lay flat to dry

Skip the dryer. Reshape while damp and dry flat to keep the fit and softness you bought it for.
3

Air it out between wears

A few hours of fresh air refreshes merino between wears — often no wash needed at all.
