Four questions, before you decide

“Won’t merino itch?”

Not ours. We knit with ultrafine merino (around 17.5 micron) — a fraction of the thickness of the coarse wool you remember. It sits smooth and soft right against bare skin, with no scratch and no undershirt needed.

“Isn’t merino a hassle to look after?”

The opposite. Merino naturally resists odour, so you wear it far longer between washes. When you do wash, turn it inside out, run a cool gentle cycle, and lay it flat to dry. No ironing — it shrugs off wrinkles on its own.

“How do I get the size right?”

Our base layers are made for a close, next-to-skin fit so they layer cleanly under anything. If you’re between sizes or want a roomier feel, size up. The size guide has exact measurements for every piece — a quick check before you order takes the guesswork out.
